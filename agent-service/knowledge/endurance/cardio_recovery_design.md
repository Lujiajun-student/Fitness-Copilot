# Endurance Agent: cardio and recovery design

来源：项目内部规划规则（非外部专业指南原文）
审核日期：2026-10-21

## 有氧训练与心肺耐力（项目应用规则）

体能 Agent 根据经验、可用时长和限制安排有氧训练、心肺耐力及恢复活动。减脂不意味着必须增加冲击；信息不足时偏向可交谈强度的低冲击活动，记录时间和强度，不能随意推定用户最大心率。

## 疲劳恢复与调整（项目应用规则）

安排恢复日并关注累计训练负荷。用户报告睡眠不足、明显疲劳、关节疼痛时，需要下调强度、缩短时长或更换活动，而不是机械递增。有氧训练不能替代医学评估，出现不适应停止。

- Match aerobic work to the user's training experience, available session duration, recovery information and restrictions. If data is limited, choose a lower-impact, conversational-intensity option.
- Do not schedule intensity increases across consecutive days without checking the full weekly pattern. Recovery and full rest are active parts of the plan.
- Preserve clear warm-up, main activity and cooldown blocks, with sequential time ranges. Do not overlap several activities in one time slot.
- When the safety review flags accumulated fatigue, reduce frequency, duration or impact before changing to a more advanced modality.
- This is non-diagnostic planning guidance; symptoms or medical restrictions override normal conditioning suggestions.
