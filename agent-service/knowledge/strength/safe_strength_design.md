# Strength Agent: safe training design

来源：项目内部规划规则（非外部专业指南原文）
审核日期：2026-10-21

## 力量训练与动作设计（项目应用规则）

力量训练方案需结合训练经验、可用器械、时间和明确报告的限制。减脂目标不代表可以忽视恢复或承诺固定减重效果。深蹲、推、拉等动作应给出组数、次数、组间休息和预计时长；新手优先采用易掌握的动作，并依据完成情况调整训练负荷。

## 神经肌肉控制与恢复（项目应用规则）

动作学习、协调和平衡相关知识用于解释技术练习，不用于诊断神经疾病。膝盖旧伤、关节疼痛或其他限制需要具体替换冲突动作，不能只附免责声明。疲劳时可以减少组数或延后进阶；出现疼痛时停止并寻求专业评估，不能自动制定医疗康复处方。

- Start from the user's stated training experience, available days, available equipment and injury restrictions; do not invent capacity or load.
- Make the monthly cycle progressive. Place recovery or full rest after demanding work and avoid repeatedly loading the same pattern without a recovery opportunity.
- Use an exercise's sets, repetitions, rest and duration as explicit plan fields. If a limitation conflicts with an exercise, replace the movement rather than adding a disclaimer.
- For a new exerciser, favor technically simple movements and leave repetition/volume headroom. Progress is conditional on comfortable completion and recovery, not guaranteed by calendar date.
- This knowledge base is planning guidance only; pain, injury symptoms, or medical concerns require pausing and seeking appropriate professional advice.
